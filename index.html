Our Destinations

Three Amazing Places, One Epic Day

We picked the best spots in Lahore just for you — click through and start getting excited!

The Ultimate Indoor Playground

Super Space

Get ready for a mind-blowing indoor adventure! Super Space is packed with thrilling rides, arcade games, trampolines and colourful play zones. Race your friends on the go-karts, collect tickets at the arcade, and jump as high as you can — all inside a safe, air-conditioned space. It is the perfect place to kick off our trip and warm up for the big day ahead.

  • Thrilling indoor rides & bumper cars
  • Arcade and video game zone with prizes
  • Safe, soft play areas for everyone
  • Air-conditioned comfort all day
Lahore's Favourite Amusement Park

Joyland

Hold on tight — Joyland is where the squeals and laughter never stop! Ride the giant Ferris wheel for amazing views of Lahore, spin on the flying chairs, brave the roller coasters and cool off on the water rides. With snack stalls, games and prizes around every corner, Joyland is the heart of our trip. Do not forget to try the classic carousel with your friends!

  • Giant Ferris wheel with city views
  • Swings, coasters and family rides
  • Flying chairs and splash water rides
  • Snack stalls, games and prizes
The Famous Beating Retreat Ceremony

Wagah Border

Our last stop is the most special one — the Wagah Border! Every evening, Pakistan Rangers perform the spectacular Beating Retreat ceremony: marching, salutes and the lowering of the flag at sunset. Bring your school flag, wear your uniform with pride and join thousands of fans chanting 'Pakistan Zindabad'! It is a proud, unforgettable moment for every Pakistani — and the perfect way to end our school trip.

  • Spectacular sunset flag ceremony
  • High-energy marching by Pakistan Rangers
  • Cheering crowds and national songs
  • A proud moment for every Pakistani
